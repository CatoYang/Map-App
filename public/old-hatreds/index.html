Shanghai, 1853

The red-turbaned rebels of the Small Swords have taken the walled city. The magistrate is dead. His yamen burns.

A mile to the north, on the mud of the riverbank, the foreigners watch from their new stone houses. Ten years ago they won the right to trade here with gunboats and opium. Now they count the refugees crowding against their gates, and they count their profits.

Upriver, a man who believes he is the younger brother of Jesus Christ has crowned himself king in Nanjing. His armies are vast, his faith is fire, and they are coming east.

And in the dark between all of them, older things are counting too.

The Chronicle

Old Hatreds is a Vampire: The Masquerade chronicle about a small group of young vampires caught in the feuds of their elders, while Shanghai is born and the Taiping war sets China on fire.

You play vampires: people who died and woke up hungry. You are stronger than you were, and you will never see the sun again. You feed on the living and hide what you are from them, because the world must never know. And you fight, every night, to stay human enough to recognise yourself.

You and the other players are a coterie: a handful of new vampires bound together, by choice or by orders, in a city that is tearing itself apart.

Everything Here Happened

The world of this chronicle is real history.

  • 1842.Britain wins the First Opium War. The Treaty of Nanking forces Shanghai open to foreign trade.
  • 1845–1849.British, American and French settlements are laid out on the marshes north of the old walled city.
  • 1851.The Taiping Rebellion begins in the south. It will become one of the deadliest wars in human history.
  • 1853.The Taiping take Nanjing. In September, the Small Swords Society seizes Shanghai's walled city.
  • 1854.Imperial troops lay siege to the walled city. Refugees flood the foreign settlements. Foreign volunteers fight the Qing army in the Battle of Muddy Flat.

You will walk these streets. You can open the map and watch the city change around you.

The Old Hatreds

The rebellion is real. The hunger, the fire and the dead in the streets are real. But for some of the Kindred of Shanghai, the revolution is only an excuse. The reason is a grudge older than the city.

The Establishment. The old vampire elite of the walled city, who ruled Shanghai's nights for generations under the traditions of the Camarilla: hierarchy, secrecy, and a Prince whose word is law. The uprising has broken them. Some are trapped inside the starving city; others plot their return from exile.

The Revolutionaries. Vampires who reject the old order and its elders, the Anarchs. Someone among them saw the Small Swords coming and put the rebellion to use. They have won the city. Now they have to hold it.

The Foreigners. A handful of wealthy vampires from the West who arrived with the gunboats. They control the docks, the guns and the opium. They say they are neutral. They are very interested in who wins.

And more arrive every night: Kindred fleeing the Taiping armies, who hunt vampires as demons; pirates, smugglers and scholars passing through the port; things that have no names yet.

They have hated each other for longer than you have been alive. You will be useful to all of them.

Your Choice

This chronicle doesn't pick a side. Your coterie does.

Will you kneel to the old order, rebuild its court and earn your place in it? Or will you burn it down and build something new from the ashes? Or will you play every side against the others and survive them all?

Whether Old Hatreds becomes a Camarilla chronicle or an Anarch one is up to you and the choices you make.

Some Things Aren't Decided Yet, On Purpose

Before the first session we'll meet for a Session Zero: an evening to build characters together and decide what kind of story we want to tell. Among other things, we'll set the dials:

Know the World of Darkness Already?

Old Hatreds uses the fifth edition (V5) rules, set in the 1850s, long before most published canon. The sects, clans and politics of Shanghai have been built for this chronicle. Bring what you know, but expect the city to surprise you.
